Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This is an early test version of the app, and this page explains in plain language what it does with your information.

The short version

  • The app is for people 21 and older in the United States.
  • You can use it as a guest. You only give us an email address if you choose to save your progress.
  • We keep what you add (swipes, journal entries, photos, notes, quiz progress) so the app can work for you.
  • Your photos are private unless you choose to share one. Sharing is a tick box, it is off by default, and you can take a photo back at any time.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete any entry, any swipe, or your whole account from inside the app (Profile, then Overview).

What we collect

  • A guest ID. The app creates one automatically so your data stays yours. It is not your name.
  • Your email address, only if you tap "Save my progress with email" or sign in. It is used to send you sign-in codes and links.
  • Your age confirmation. We store that you confirmed you are 21 or older, and when. We do not ask for or store your date of birth.
  • What you do in the app. Wines you swipe on; journal entries (the wine, your verdict, your ratings of acidity, body, tannin or CO₂, sweetness and oak, the date, price, food, occasion and notes); photos you add; quiz answers and timed rounds; your trophies; and the palate summary worked out from these.
  • Feedback you send, such as a report that a wine or a quiz question looks wrong, with any note you write.
  • Technical information. Your browser keeps a sign-in token and a few settings in its own storage on your device. The companies that run the services below also see technical details such as your IP address when your device connects to them.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your journal and swipes, learn your palate, track your quiz progress, and sign you in.
  • To improve the wine information and quiz questions, using the feedback people send.
  • To keep the app working and safe.

Who else handles it

We use these services to run the app. They handle your information only to provide their service.

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in system, and private storage for your photos.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Brevo: delivers the sign-in emails, only if you use email sign-in.
  • esm.sh and jsDelivr: deliver a piece of code the app needs. They see your IP address and browser details when it loads.

The people who run this app can technically access the database for maintenance and support. Each person's data is protected by rules that stop other users from reading it, and photos are kept in private storage. The exception is a photo you choose to share (see the next section).

Photos you choose to share

When you add a photo to a journal entry for a wine in our catalog, you can tick a box to share it with other players. It is off unless you tick it.

  • What happens. The photo is offered to our editors. They see the picture and the wine only: not your name, email, notes, price or anything else in your journal. An editor either approves it or rejects it. A rejected photo is never shown to anyone.
  • If it is approved. The photo may appear on that wine's card for every player, and may be copied to other vintages of the same wine. It is shown without your name.
  • Taking it back. You can stop sharing a photo at any time from the entry. The shared copies are deleted. They are also deleted if you delete the photo, the journal entry, or your account. An editor can also take a shared photo down.
  • Please only share photos of the bottle. Do not share photos that show people, children, other personal information or anything you do not have the right to share. Editors may reject a photo for any reason.
  • By ticking the box you confirm you took the photo and agree that we may show it in the app as described here. Photos that are not shared stay private.

Deleting your information

  • Delete one journal entry or one swipe. The entry is removed from your account together with its notes, food, occasion and photos, including any copies of its photos you had shared.
  • Delete your whole account. In the app, open Profile, then Overview, then "Delete my account and all my data". This erases your account, sign-in, email address, journal, notes, photos (including any you had shared), swipes, quiz history, trophies and palate.
  • What stays. When you delete entries or an account, we keep an anonymous record of the structured part of what you rated or swiped: the wine, the verdict, the structure ratings, the price and the month. It has no name, email, photos or notes. It is filed under a one-way code so that if you add the same wine again, the new entry replaces it. We do not use it to identify you.
  • We do not currently delete inactive accounts automatically.

Your choices

You can use the app as a guest, change or delete what you add, or delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your information. Write to us at the address below and we will help.

Age

The app is for people 21 and older in the United States. If we learn that someone under 21 has been using it, we will delete their account.

Changes

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its date.
