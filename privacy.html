Terms and privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. DRAFT, being reviewed by our lawyers, so the wording may change. This is an early test version of the app. This one page is our terms and conditions and our privacy policy, in plain language: the rules for using the app and what it does with your information. By tapping "I accept the terms and conditions" you agree to all of it.

The short version

  • The app is for people 21 and older in the United States.
  • You can use it as a guest. You only give us an email address if you choose to save your progress.
  • We keep what you add (swipes, journal entries, photos, notes, quiz progress, game answers) so the app can work for you, and we use it for research into how tastes relate to the wines people enjoy.
  • Photos you add to a wine in our catalog are offered to our editors and, once approved, may appear on that wine's card without your name (you agree to this on the consent page when you start). You can take any photo back at any time. Photos of wines you type in yourself stay private.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • You can delete any entry, swipe or photo, or your whole account, from inside the app. Deleting makes things disappear for you, but what you added is kept without your name (see "Deleting your information").

Using the app

  • This is an early test version, provided as it is. It is for fun and for learning about wine, for your own use.
  • It is not health or medical advice, and please drink responsibly.
  • We may change or stop the app, and we may remove content that breaks these terms.
  • You keep ownership of what you add. You give us a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence to store, use, copy, adapt, analyse and display it, in a form that does not name you, to run and improve the app and for research. This continues after you delete things or your account, because what you add is kept (see "Deleting your information").

What we collect

  • A guest ID. The app creates one automatically so your data stays yours. It is not your name.
  • Your email address, only if you tap "Save my progress with email" or sign in. It is used to send you sign-in codes and links.
  • Your age confirmation. We store that you confirmed you are 21 or older, and when. We do not ask for or store your date of birth.
  • What you do in the app. Wines you swipe on; journal entries (the wine, your verdict, how it tasted, the ratings of acidity, body, tannin, sweetness and oak that the app works out from your answers, the date, price, food, occasion and notes); photos you add; quiz answers and timed rounds; your trophies; and the palate summary worked out from these.
  • Your game answers. In games such as This or That you pick between two foods or everyday things. We save your picks and which games and cards you finish.
  • Feedback you send, such as a report that a wine or a quiz question looks wrong, with any note you write.
  • Technical information. Your browser keeps a sign-in token and a few settings in its own storage on your device. The companies that run the services below also see technical details such as your IP address when your device connects to them.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your journal and swipes, learn your palate, track your quiz progress, and sign you in.
  • To improve the wine information and quiz questions, using the feedback people send.
  • To keep the app working and safe.
  • For research. To study how what people like to eat, drink and do relates to the wines they enjoy, and to make the app's suggestions better. We may publish or share what we find only as combined results about many people, never about one person.

Games

In games such as This or That you choose between two foods or everyday things. Wine is not named in the questions, and there are no right or wrong answers. Your picks are saved with your account, are used for the research above, and are used for the suggestions shown in the game. They do not change the wines shown to you in Discover.

Who else handles it

We use these services to run the app. They handle your information only to provide their service.

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in system, and private storage for your photos.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Brevo: delivers the sign-in emails, only if you use email sign-in.
  • esm.sh and jsDelivr: deliver a piece of code the app needs. They see your IP address and browser details when it loads.

The people who run this app can technically access the database for maintenance and support. Each person's data is protected by rules that stop other users from reading it, and photos are kept in private storage. The exception is a photo of a catalog wine, which is offered for sharing (see the next section).

How it tasted

When you rate a wine, you answer a few everyday questions: whether the wine tasted as expected, what stood out (for example sour, fruity, sweet, thin, heavy or drying) and how much, and whether you would drink it alone or with food. The app turns your answers into structure ratings for that bottle. They are private to you, are used only to work out your own palate, and disappear from your view when you delete the entry. We keep a copy without your name (see "Deleting your information").

Your own version of a wine

You can change how a wine in the catalog is described for you (its type, grapes, place, name or vintage). The change is private to you: other players and the catalog are not affected. It disappears from your view when you delete the entry (we keep a copy without your name), and you can go back to the catalog's details at any time.

Professional tasting notes

Some accounts have a professional tasting grid. The notes you make with it (appearance, nose, palate and conclusions) belong to your journal entry. They are private to you, are not shared with anyone, and disappear from your view when you delete the entry. We keep a copy without your name (see "Deleting your information").

Prices

The price you enter for a bottle in your journal stays in your journal. It is also added, without your name, to the other prices entered for the same wine, so the app can show a typical price on that wine's card. An average is used only once at least three different players have entered a price for the wine, and it is blended with a price set by our editors. Nobody can see an individual player's price.

Photos of catalog wines

When you start, the consent page asks you to agree that photos you add to journal entries for wines in our catalog may be shown to other players. There is no tick box on each photo. Photos of wines you type in yourself are never shared.

  • What happens. The photo is offered to our editors. They see the picture and the wine only: not your name, email, notes, price or anything else in your journal. An editor either approves it or rejects it. A rejected photo is never shown to anyone.
  • If it is approved. The photo may appear on that wine's card for every player, and may be copied to other vintages of the same wine. It is shown without your name.
  • Taking it back. You can stop sharing a photo at any time: tap Shared under the photo in the entry. The shared copies are deleted. They are also deleted if you delete the photo, the journal entry, or your account. An editor can also take a shared photo down.
  • Please only add photos of the bottle. Do not add photos that show people, children, other personal information or anything you do not have the right to share. Editors may reject a photo for any reason.
  • By accepting the consent page you confirm that photos you add are yours to share and agree that we may show them in the app as described here.

Deleting your information

  • Delete a journal entry, a swipe or a photo. It disappears from your account and your screens, and any copies of its photos you had shared are taken down for other players. We keep a copy in our records, without your name, and use it only as described on this page.
  • Delete your whole account. In the app, open Profile, then Overview, then "Delete my account". This erases your account, sign-in, email address, profile, consent record, trophies and palate summary, and takes back any photos you had shared for the catalog.
  • What stays. Everything else you added stays: your swipes, journal entries and notes, photos, wines you typed in, private changes to wines, quiz answers, game answers and feedback. It is kept without your name or email, and nothing links it to your account. It is filed under a random code, so it stays together as one anonymous person's data. We keep it for research and to improve the app, and we do not use it to identify you.
  • What this means for you. Once your account is deleted we cannot find your data again or show it to you, because the link to you is gone. If something we keep is harmful, unlawful or shows private information about someone, write to us and we will review it. Notes and photos can show things about a person, so please do not add anything you would not want kept. Photos are kept in private storage, and the people who run this app can access them for maintenance, support and research.
  • We do not currently delete inactive accounts automatically.

Your choices

You can use the app as a guest, change or delete what you add, or delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your information. Write to us at the address below and we will help.

Age
